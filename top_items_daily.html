Estimated Competitor Sales

Row-level model that uses matched TENKI sales to estimate missing ranked competitor sales from the Rakuten ranking files.

Model range
Matched TENKI Sales - Ranking rows with actual TENKI sales
Estimated Competitor Sales - Predicted missing ranked rows
Estimated Ranked Market - TENKI matches plus competitor estimates
Ranking Coverage - Matched vs predicted ranking rows
Holdout WMAPE - Hidden known ranked rows
Median APE - Median row-level percentage error
Within 25% - Predictions within 25% error
Actual TENKI sales Projected competitor shops

Model method: matched TENKI ranking rows train a log-sales model using genre, rank, rank bucket, price band, weekday, month, event context, price, and recent genre-sales level. Validation repeats hidden 5% known-row trials, and the chart separates the top projected competitor shops instead of summing them into one line.
