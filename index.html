Actionable Recommendations

Business actions suggested by the ranking, event, traffic, and forecast views.

Protect high-rank visibilityKeep rank 1-10 placements active during months where sales and PV rise together, especially around major event windows.
Separate traffic problems from conversion problemsIf PV rises but CVR stays flat, improve product pages or pricing. If CVR rises but PV is weak, prioritize exposure and campaign placement.
Use December differentlyDecember behaves unlike normal months. Treat it as a separate planning period for inventory, ranking placement, and event timing.
Investigate negative-lift eventsSome events may be scheduled during slow periods or attracting low-intent traffic. Compare PV and CVR before deciding to cut them.
